## Challenge

**Town Tour 02** (100 pts) gave a photo of a business storefront. The goal was to identify a nearby chain supermarket implied by the challenge description and submit its name in the flag format.

## Approach

1. Identify the brand in the photo — a **Vinaphone** / VNPT telecommunications location in Vietnam
2. Translate the main board text — little useful signal for geolocation
3. Reverse-image search the photo; land on a Vietnamese news article with the same building from another angle: [VNPT Hà Nội – Telecommunications Center 8](https://langngheviet.com.vn/vnpt-ha-noi-trung-tam-vien-thong-8-gop-phan-tich-cuc-vao-su-phat-trien-va-doi-moi-cua-nganh-3877.html)
4. Translate the image subtitle in that article — it placed the site as a **Service Business Point of VNPT Hanoi’s Telecommunications Center 8 in Sơn Tây Town**
5. Search Vinaphone / VNPT locations in Sơn Tây and compare Map / Street View angles until the building matched the challenge photo
6. Search nearby chain supermarkets; one was about a minute away and fit the brief
7. Format the mart name as the flag and submit

Brand recognition got the country and industry; reverse image search plus caption translation got the town; map verification got the exact building; proximity + “chain supermarket” got the answer.

## Takeaways

- OCR/translate of signage is worth a try, but reverse image search often finds the same facade faster
- News captions and photo credits are high-signal geolocation sources — translate subtitles, not only headlines
- Once you have a town, verify candidates visually in Maps before hunting secondary POIs
- Challenge constraints (“nearby”, “chain”) shrink the search around a confirmed pin
