## Challenge

**Missing Friend** (100 pts) provided images, including a MotoGP-related photo with a **PERTAMINA** sign and a separate food/bar photo. The brief implied a real place tied to that event context.

## Approach

1. Pull readable text from the MotoGP image — `PERTAMINA` stood out
2. Search `PERTAMINA MotoGP 2025` and identify the **PERTAMINA Grand Prix of Indonesia 2025**
3. Try venue maps for the circuit — no useful match to the bar photo
4. Try after-party / event nightlife searches — also a dead end
5. Pivot: search bars near the track area and visually compare to the attached food/bar image
6. Match **Cantina Mexicana Kuta Lombok** to the photo
7. Copy the Google Maps **Plus Code** for that place and submit it as the flag

Visual confirmation beat map hunting. Once the venue matched, the Plus Code was sitting on the Maps listing.

## Takeaways

- Extract brand/event text from photos before guessing locations
- Dead ends (venue maps, after-parties) are useful — they tell you to change the query, not quit
- Side-by-side image matching against local businesses is a standard geolocation move
- Google Maps Plus Codes are compact, location-specific strings that fit CTF flag formats well
